Place, with drawing

Satellite opens by default. On the screen's left edge is a key for each drawing tool, and the readout beside it shows the length or area as you draw. Your drawings go into named maps, listed at the top of the left column. On the right are the 7 cells for the satellite's buildings, plus downloads and OSM. The then-and-now pair now sits in the lower band. Press the keys to see the readout change. Hide either side with its arrow, or both with the map-only key at the screen's top right; the key under it goes full screen. All figures are placeholders.
