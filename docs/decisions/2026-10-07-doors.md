# The five doors on the node

**Question.** The redesign in planetai-design (R33 to R41, `prototypes/instrument/` on `place-map-2026-10-07`) draws
the node as five doors from a hand-built capture of node #1. Can it run on a node, from the node's own routes,
without the page computing what the node did not say, and without a screen seeing more than `SHARE_LEVEL` gives it?

**Status.** Built behind `/?layout=doors`, 7 October 2026, on branch `doors-2026-10-07` (from the v0.80 map
branch). The dashboard at `/` is unchanged. Tomas asked for "the dashboard release" the same day; it ships after
v0.80, alone, because `docs/NEXT_RELEASE.md` rule 3 allows one large page change per release.

## How

- **One object, from the routes.** The prototype's door files are copied as they were (`app/static/doors-*.js`); only
  what they read changed. `doors-load.js` asks `/issues`, `/issues/days?days=7`, `/health`, `/rho`, `/earth`,
  `/reach`, `/sensors`, `/observations`, `/cells`, `/stats` and `/settings`, plus `/aggregates`, `/actions` and
  `/alerts` when the screen holds a token, and `buildD()` renames and pivots them into the prototype's shape.
  On node #1's capture it reproduced the prototype's `data.json` value for value, apart from rounding and the
  node's point, which is `/health`'s three decimals and not the capture's seven. The flow the Wall and Node replay is
  `flowFrom()`, from the same answers; it reproduced `flow.json` exactly. `tests/test_doors.py` keeps both honest.
- **Token-only stays token-only.** `/geo/*`, `/ground/*`, `/place/geojson`, `/aggregates` and `/actions` answer no
  screen without the token at any level (Tomas, 7 Oct: keep `/ground` private). The doors draw what is left and
  say what is not drawn: the rose places no station, the Place map draws no grid or base, the Data door has no
  hourly table, the flow counts nothing (its alerts and answers come from `/issues`' asks). Node → Unlock stores a
  token in that browser.
- **Invented copy went.** The prototype's "share level off" reads `SHARE_LEVEL`; "The diamond is Fab Lab Bali" reads
  the facility's name; the radio's rate is `announce_s`; the satellite years are the node's. Another node's point is
  given to one decimal. The Bali land mask and the design kit's plan are not shipped; Natural Earth's 1:110m land is,
  for the Node door's world (NOTICE).
- **Written back.** Done, Not now and Doesn't fit post to `/actions` with the event's id, as the dashboard does, and
  only for an event still open at the live hour. The ask pane posts to `/ask` and prints the answer's words.

## R42: the Wall as a living map, and Now on a map (7 Oct, later)

Tomas: the Wall's flow by scale "does not work"; Now's "around this house" should be a vector map that keeps the
rose's rings and gains the hex grid. A survey of the deck.gl, MapLibre, d3 and Plot galleries (trips, scatterplot with
a time filter, webgl-wind and earth.nullschool, AQICN's wind-against-PM2.5 study; horizon and ridgeline as the calmer
alternatives) chose a living map for the Wall: the node's dark street map, the wind now drifting over it, every
station a disc whose area is that hour's mean, the last 6 or 24 hours replayed in 30 s, and a ring leaving the house
for each alert out (red) or answer back (green) in the hour (`doors-wallmap.js`). No surface is painted between
stations: twelve stations over 15 km would be an invented field. Now's map draws the stations at the points
`/sensors` gives, their 15-minute means, the node's rings at 0.5, 2, 5 and 15 km and the resolution-7 cells
(`/geo/rings`, `/geo/grid`, token-only) with the node's own res-8 cell solid. The flow by scale stays on the Node door.

Found on node #1: `/aggregates?hours=24` carries forecast rows up to 48 hours ahead, so the replay first played
tomorrow. The loader now stops the table at the hour `/issues` was computed.
