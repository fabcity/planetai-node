# The flows pack — decision

**Question.** The Index formula is FCI = DIDO · (1 − PITO) · ρ, and no node has ever logged a single thing a place
made, used, fixed or took in. `Economic|Community` is filled by a proxy, businesses per km², marked `partial`
(`packs/place`), and the production and trade cells are the pinned gap (awesome-fabcity-data#42). Two reads on
28 September 2026, of Interfacer and of Sensorica, both ended in the same sentence: take the ValueFlows
vocabulary, run neither platform. So: how does a lab or a household log what it makes, in a way the node can turn
into cells, without a new table, a new service, a partner, or a week of a person's time?

**Recommendation.** Flows as readings. A logged line becomes one `readings` row on one `sensors` row per logger
(`flow:<NODE>`, `source='flows'`, `kind='survey'`, `local=TRUE`), and the metric is the whole event:
`<action>.<class>.<ring>`. The action is a ValueFlows verb, unchanged. The class comes from a 30-entry vocabulary,
`packs/flows/resources.yml`, which carries what does not vary per line: the unit, the nature of the thing
(physical, digital, information, service), the pillar, the planetary-boundary ceiling it draws on, and the
doughnut foundation it counts toward. The ring is where it came from or went to, in the Index's own scales:
`cell`, `city`, `region`, `beyond`. The value is the quantity in the class's unit. Nothing else is stored: no note,
no actor beyond the logger row, no address. A `POST /flows` refuses anything not in the vocabulary, a `/log`
line to the Telegram bot and an MCP `log_flow` tool call it, and `packs/flows/cells.yml` sums the rows into
Community cells, all `partial`, because a survey is not an instrument. Two `WHERE source <> 'flows'` clauses keep
the rows out of the child push and the daily export, so this quarter a flow leaves the node only as a cell.

**Cost.** One data pack (`pack.yaml`, `resources.yml`, `cells.yml`, `README.md`), about thirty lines for
`POST /flows` beside `/actions` in `app/main.py`, one MCP tool in `app/agent.py`, one `/log` branch in
`app/agent_loop.py` copied from `/act`, two WHERE clauses, one assertion block in `tools/check_rules.py` (a
metric whose class is not in the vocabulary fails lint), one test. No migration: `sensors` and `readings` hold it
as they are, and `kind='survey'` already exists. No dependency. About a week of one maintainer; Fab Lab Bali can
log on day three.

**Unblocks.** The first real production numbers in the Index, from the place that made them. The thirty-day test
that both reads named as the first move: one lab logging what it makes for a month in under five minutes a day. If
that holds, the vocabulary holds. If it does not, no platform would have fixed it. And the make pack's other half:
a facility with a point becomes a facility with a month of `produce` rows behind it.

## §1 · The grammar

```
<action>.<class>.<ring>        value in the class's unit        e.g.  produce.filter.cell  3
                                                                     consume.filament.beyond  1.2
                                                                     modify.appliance.cell  1
                                                                     cite.design.beyond  1
```

| part | values | from |
|---|---|---|
| action | `produce` `consume` `use` `work` `modify` `transfer` `cite` `raise` `lower` | ValueFlows, verbatim. A repair is `modify`; a design taken in is `cite`; `raise`/`lower` are an inventory that changed without a process, for living things |
| class | 30 keys in `resources.yml` | ours. Each carries unit, nature, pillar, ceiling, foundation, mfa; living classes carry `yields` |
| ring | `cell` `city` `region` `beyond` | the Index's scales. For `consume`/`cite`, where it came from; for `transfer`, where it went; for `produce`/`modify`/`use`/`work`, where it happened, almost always `cell` |

The vocabulary is a contract: a class, once used, is never renamed or given a new unit, because a renamed class
orphans every row logged under the old one. A class may be added in a minor release. The action list changes only
with a `docs/decisions/` entry. The ring list does not change; it is the Index's.

The Telegram grammar is looser and maps onto the strict one: `/log made 3 filter`, `/log used 1.2 filament
beyond`, `/log fixed 1 appliance`, `/log worked 3 work_hours`, `/log took 1 design beyond`, `/log sent 4
waste_residual beyond`, `/log planted 40 plant`, `/log born 6 animal`, `/log lost 1 animal`. `made` and
`planted` → produce, `used` → consume for a physical class and `use` for a service, `fixed` → modify, `worked` →
work, `took` → cite, `sent` → transfer, `born` → raise, `lost` → lower. Ring defaults to `cell`. The bot answers with the strict
metric it wrote, so a person learns the real grammar by reading their own line back.

## §2 · Where the three frameworks land

**PITO → DIDO is two columns.** The ring says where; the class's `nature` says what kind of thing. Physical from
`beyond` is a product in: PITO. Digital or information from anywhere is data in, and physical made in the cell is
product out: DIDO. The ring alone cannot tell a design file from a finished chair, both arriving from Hamburg; the
nature can. Over quarters, a place whose `consume.<physical>.beyond` share falls while its `cite.design.*` and
`produce.<physical>.cell` counts rise is a place making the transition, and the cells show it without anyone
computing a score on the node.

**Planetary boundaries are a tag and, later, a budget from above.** Every class names the ceiling it draws on: the
nine boundaries by short name, plus `materials` for the material-footprint ceiling the Index already uses, kept
apart because a tonne of steel is not a planetary boundary and should not be read as one. This quarter the node
tags and totals: physical mass consumed in thirty days, and its `beyond` share. It does not score against a
budget, because no tier publishes one yet. ARCHITECTURE.md §2 says the bioregion and planet tiers enter as
boundary conditions published downward, never as roll-ups; the per-place budget is theirs to publish, and the
day one arrives the cell divides by it. Inventing a per-lab share of eight tonnes per capita today would put a
number on the wall nobody can defend.

**The doughnut is the foundation tag.** Food grown, water made available, electricity made on the roof, hours of
teaching, a filter that clears a room: each class that meets a need names the social foundation it counts
toward, and `Social|Community` sums them by unit. Self-reported, so `partial`; `live` stays reserved for a
measured flow, which is what a machine on MQTT will give the same table later.

## §3 · The cells

All in `packs/flows/cells.yml`, all `state: partial`, all over the last 30 days, all read only rows where
`source = 'flows'` and custody holds.

| cell | unit | what the SQL sums |
|---|---|---|
| `Economic\|Community` | % of physical inputs by mass from the cell or city | `consume` rows of `kg` classes: (`cell` + `city`) / all rings |
| `Economic\|Community` | things made or fixed | `produce` + `modify` rows of `count` classes |
| `Economic\|Community` | designs taken in | `cite.design.*` |
| `Social\|Community` | kg of food grown | `produce.food.cell` |
| `Social\|Community` | litres of water made available | `produce.water.cell` |
| `Social\|Community` | kWh made here | `produce.electricity.cell` |
| `Social\|Community` | hours taught or attended | `work.training_hours.*` |
| `Environmental\|Community` | kg of physical inputs consumed | `consume` rows of `kg` classes, all rings |
| `Environmental\|Community` | % of that mass from beyond | `consume` `kg` classes, `beyond` / all |

Units never mix inside one cell: a share is over `kg` classes only, a count is over `count` classes only. A class
in `hour`, `litre` or `kWh` gets its own row. The registry's `cells/economic-community.yaml` gains these as
`minimum_indicators` in a separate PR to awesome-fabcity-data, with `does_not_claim` extended: a self-reported
log is what one lab said it did, not what a district produced.

## §4 · What leaves the node, and what does not

- Cells leave, through `GET /cells`, as they do for every pack: a value, a unit, a state, a source string.
- Rows do not. `push_aggregates` and `export` both read `readings_1h`, which would carry every flow upstream and
  onto IPFS as an hourly mean of kilograms. Both get `WHERE s.source <> 'flows'`. When a parent exists and wants
  monthly totals, that is a `flows-v0` wire document, additive, in ValueFlows names so any VF reader can take it,
  and it is not written until the parent exists.
- Nothing about a person leaves. There is no note column, and that is a feature: a household's own sentences stay
  in the Telegram chat that carried them. The logger row is `flow:<NODE>`, not a name.

## §5 · The ceiling of A, and the upgrade

This is the lazy shape and it has a known ceiling. It cannot say what went into what (no process link between
`consume.filament.beyond` and `produce.filter.cell`), it cannot name a machine (`machine_hours` is one class until a
resources table exists), it cannot carry a note, and `readings_1h`'s mean is meaningless for it, which is why the
cells read `readings` directly. The day a lab asks "what went into this chair", or a parent wants monthly totals,
the upgrade is an additive `flows` table with `resource_id`, `process`, `nature`, `ring` as columns and the same
vocabulary behind it. Nothing built here is thrown away then: the vocabulary, the grammar, the bot line and the
cells stay, and the table is a second writer.

Not taken, on purpose: a resources table with Nondominium's Layer 0 fields (custodian, property regime,
lifecycle stage), a `flows-v0` wire doc, a partner contract on the vocabulary. The verbs are ValueFlows', which is
a public specification, and using them binds this node to nobody.

## §6 · The spec, read (29 September)

ValueFlows 1.0.0 was tagged on 24 February 2026, CC BY-SA 4.0, last commit 1 September 2026, with a stated
backward-compatibility promise on the core. Sixteen implementations, three serious. The nine verbs here are
the spec's, checked against its behaviour table in the authoritative Turtle, with three footnotes and one
addition:

- **`modify` alone is a denormalisation.** In the spec a repair is `accept` (input) paired with `modify`
  (output) on a process. The flat row keeps `modify`; a wire document expands it. The spec permits this in so
  many words: more normalised than a database needs, and the specified shape only for data provided for
  interoperability.
- **`use` carries two quantities in the spec** (resource and effort); `use.machine_hours` carries one. On the
  wire the value maps to `effortQuantity` and the machine to the resource.
- **`transfer` needs a receiver agent in the spec**; ours has a ring. On the node that is fine; a wire document
  either names a placeholder receiver or writes `move`, which is location-only.
- **`mfa` is added to every class**: the Eurostat EW-MFA bin (biomass, metal ores, non-metallic minerals,
  fossil energy, or none), so the Environmental tonnage rolls up to what the Index compares regions against.
- **Living things are classes** (decided 29 September): `plant`, `animal`, `habitat` (in `m2`, the sixth
  unit), with `raise` and `lower` as the eighth and ninth verbs, so the biosphere ceiling is drawn on from the
  giving side and the Environmental cell can count what a place gave back, not only what it consumed. The
  relation between a living thing and a material (a tree and its timber) is a `yields:` list on the living
  class, ValueFlows' knowledge layer in one line; a harvest is two rows, unlinked, and the ring carries the
  accounting. Nature as a named agent waits for the wire document's provider and receiver.
- **`goods` is the thirtieth class** (29 September): a finished product with no better class, counted, so a shelf
  survey and a household's purchases have somewhere to land. Its `ceiling` is `materials`, its `mfa` is none: a
  product is counted, not weighed, and its material bin is unknown at the shelf. `bicycle` was renamed
  `micromobility` on 30 September (bicycles, e-bikes, scooters, light electric vehicles), before any place had
  logged it, which is the only time a rename is allowed.
- **The recording mechanism is a future step** (30 September), P3 of the plan, after the typed month at Bali:
  the photo as the default log and the typed line as the fallback, the photo's own location snapped to the cell
  and the facility then stripped, a local vision model that drafts class and quantity for a person to confirm
  (the ring is always the person's word), and the node asking the ground to confirm what the sky saw, as an
  alert of kind *confirm* whose reply is an action. Nothing read from a photo is ever `live`, an unconfirmed
  draft is never a row, and no image is stored. Designed in *Mapping a cell's flows*; not built.

Alternatives were read against five criteria (stable, readable by others' tools, open licence, no single
vendor, loggable in five minutes): GS1 EPCIS/CBV, UN/CEFACT UNTP, W3C PROV-O, Schema.org, Murmurations,
EW-MFA and ISO 59020, OKH/OKW, the ESPR and CEN DPP work. None meets all five; EPCIS and PROV-O each get an
alignment table in `packs/flows/README.md` instead of a switch. ValueFlows is not forked: a fork splits two
maintainers into one and one and loses every reader. The vocabulary is published as a **profile**,
`fabcity-flows` 1.0, CC BY 4.0, at planetai.fab.city/vocab/flows/1.0/, maintained in this repo.
