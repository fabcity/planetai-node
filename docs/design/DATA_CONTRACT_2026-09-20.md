Tomas asked that the design land on the node's data structure as it is, not as the sketch guessed it from screenshots and docs. Nine releases shipped between the sketch's first round and this reading (v0.58 → v0.66, 18–20 Sep). Most of them moved toward the design. Three moved the vocabulary under it, and the last two put a new kind of thing on the wire — a place — that the sketch had no slot for. Here is the ledger.

## 1 · What changed since the sketch started, and what it does to the design

**v0.58 — the page polls again.** `POLL_SECONDS` (300 s on node #1), re-fetching `/issues`, `/health`, `/rho` only; a poll re-binds and redraws without re-fetching tiles, the plan, a setting or a satellite year; a missed poll leaves the figures and drops the `live` pill, the stamp says how long the node has been silent. *Design consequence:* the **asking** state plays at boot, on ↻ and on reconnect after silence — never on a poll. A poll landing is `reading-fade` on the numerals that changed and nothing else. The ground is a function of cell · resolution · base · register and a poll touches none of them: the request ledger's tile count must not tick on a poll.

**v0.59 — the dial belongs to Now; the hero leads with what moved.** The rail draws only on Now (and Arrange, which is Now in a mode); the sketch already has it inside the Now view, correct. The headline rule is now: state wins outright; inside a state, the issue that moved most in three hours (`moved`, unsigned, a proportion of the issue's own recent level); an exact tie goes to `NODE_ISSUES`. The rule is printed under the grain line in three languages. *Consequence:* the sketch's rule sentence is replaced (done, v9 of the artifact); the digest and the learn mark `states` quote the new rule.

**v0.61 — the registry rides inside the node; four new packs; the satellite player works again.** `GET /sources` and `planetai sources` answer from a snapshot of `awesome-fabcity-data` (209 entries then); v0.64 makes the last column an `adapter` string (`core:ckan`, `pack:coast`) instead of a hand-ticked boolean. New packs: `season` (the ring's last seven days against its own sixty, a rule, no fetch), `xiaomi-air` (a Mi purifier on the LAN as indoor sensors: PM2.5, temp, RH, **filter life** — the first household appliance the node reads), `thingdata` (repair commons as observations, idle unless named). The AlphaEarth player draws one year at a time again; the Sentinel strip is unchanged. *Consequence:* Network gains a real row — *what this place could read* — and Set up's Sources group has a source of truth; the `xiaomi-air` filter-life reading is the first "capacity" datum the act layer has been waiting for (19 Sep act-side note §5) and belongs in Act as a line, not in Observe as a sensor.

**v0.63 — schema keys; `AGENT_PREFER=private`; three languages; arm64.** `/issues` carries `"schema": "issues-v0"`; a reader that meets an unknown version draws what it recognises and prints one sentence. *Consequence:* the page asserts `issues-v0` (14 Sep prompt already asked; it is now real).

**v0.65 — the node can say where to go, not only what is wrong.** Two things. The registry is re-pinned to `85a194c`, **217 entries**, and eight of them carry `role: [observe, act]` with an `act_kind` — two directories of fab labs (`facility`), five libraries of open designs (`design`), the list of pledged Fab Cities (`network`) — each with its `license` text as the publisher states it; three are openly licensed (Appropedia, OSHWA, Things That Work), five are not, and each says so in its own row. Nothing reads the eight yet except the second thing: a new code pack, **`make`**, which keeps the active fab labs within `MAKE_RADIUS_KM` (50) of the node as `sensors` rows with `kind='facility'`, `local=False`, no readings, no metric, no cell, no rule (`rules.yml` is a comment explaining why), refreshed every `MAKE_REFRESH_DAYS` (30, the archive's own cadence) from the Fab Foundation's dated monthly snapshots (`MAKE_SNAPSHOT` pins one so a fleet answers identically). Its one contribution to language is `ask_line(rows, locale)` — "Fab Lab Bali, 17.2 km (laser cutting, 3D printing, CNC milling)", machine names in en · id · es — which `app/report.py` appends to the report as *Nearest place to make or fix something*. `rules.yml` says where that line belongs: to the asks in *other* packs' rules, "the moment you have been told you need something made". *Consequence:* the Act stage gains its second household-side line after filter life, and the first that is a **place** rather than a reading. It comes with two constraints the sketch did not have: the facility rows reach the page only through `/sensors` (a place has no readings, so it is absent from `stats` and therefore from `/issues.stations`); and the sentence exists only in the report — `/issues` does not carry it (**gap F**, below). The registry row for Network grows a second line: how many of the 217 are places to act, and how many of those are openly licensed.

**v0.66 — the pack behind its own switch.** `MAKE_ENABLED=0` ships as the default, because an empty `PACKS_ENABLED` means every pack, so on a node that already allowed code packs `make` turned itself on at update and read 5.3 MB before anyone chose it — v0.65's release note had said otherwise, and this fixed it the same day. The reason a pack gets its own switch when no other does: the directory is not openly licensed, and the Foundation's decision to read it covers the Foundation, not each node's keeper. *Consequence:* on a fresh node and on node #1 as shipped, there are **no facility rows**; the page must draw the absence as a sentence naming the switch and the reason, in the node's words (`/settings` help text from `pack.yaml`), and the fixture for the pack (prompt 0) is taken on v0.66 so it looks like what testers have. A second consequence for Network: at pin `85a194c` the `fablabs-io` entry's `adapter` is `null` although `pack:make` reads it — the registry's CI checks adapter strings against this repo and the pack landed the same day as the pin. The page reads `adapter` and never infers, so "read" says 14 until the registry is re-pinned upstream; that is a re-pin owed, recorded in the handoff, not a page bug.

## 2 · The `/issues` document as it is

Top level: `schema` · `order` (declared issues) · `undeclared` · `dropped` · `headline` · `as_of` · `distances` (`room, yard, ring, region`) · `labels` (those four words in `en`/`id`/`es`) · `issues{}` · `stations[]` (every station with a coordinate *and a reading*: `km` from the node, cell, `local`, `kind`, name, series — facilities are not here) · `metrics{}` (unit, dp, issue, label per metric) · `asks` (the act ledger: `acts[]` first lines, `actions[]` with `stage` ∈ acknowledged · acted · measured, `levels{act,warn,info}`) · `mesh` · `geometry{}` (`publication`, `ladder` res 0–13, `nav` plates res 2–12 with two rings of cells and their contents, `grain_table`, `claims`, `radio`, `settings`, `source`).

Per issue: `state` · `watched` · `reason` + `reason_text{loc}` (the kicker) · `name` · `kind` (sensed | context) · `metric` · `unit` · `dp` · `headline` (which distance carries the numeral) · `stack{room,yard,ring,region}` each `{value, n, source, provenance, age…}` or null · `line` · `attribution` (clear · inside · everywhere · outside_worse · mixed · unknown) · `trend` · `moved` · `open_asks[]` · `series{distance}` + `buckets[]` (24 hourly) · `readouts[]` (context issues' figures with comparison or reason) · `provenance[]` (every figure with source and word — the Figures ledger, already computed) · `sentence{loc}`.

`grain_table` per res 2–12: `res, home, area_m2, edge_m, occupied, in_my_cell, mine_in_my_cell, may_leave, finer_than_published`. That is the rail, the log rule, the zones, and the whole of Decide's *grain that holds*, already on the wire.

Beside it, two routes the plate now needs that it did not before: **`/sensors`** (every `sensors` row with `custody`, `kind`, `meta` — since v0.65 this includes `kind='facility'` rows with `meta.{distance_km, capabilities, url, snapshot, fetched, attribution, registry_slug}` when `make` is on) and **`/sources`** (the registry's `index.json` as-is: `slug, name, pillar, scale, cell, status, license, role, act_kind, adapter, pilot_relevance, tags, added, updated, url, api, auth`, plus a `REGISTRY_VERSION` with `sha`, `synced`, `entries`).

## 4 · Node-side additions the page needs (six, all small), in the order the prompts want them

- **A · `digest`** — four sentences per locale in `/issues`, computed from data already in the bundle (prompt 1, STOP to show templates first).
- **B · fifteen-minute series** — `GET /series?metric=pm25&hours=24&step=15m` from `readings` (not `readings_1h`), room only; or the barcode draws 24 hourly bars and says so (prompt 3 decides; default: hourly today, 15-min as a one-line follow-up).
- **C · the funnel** — `/rho` gains `funnel: {asked, acknowledged, acted, measured}` counts and `latency_minutes` medians between consecutive stages, from `alerts` × `actions` (prompt 3; the ρ definition does not change).
- **D · ρ observed** — stays with the review pack's prompt 9 (spec first); the page draws the word until then.
- **E · reach** — oldest bucket per source kind, three numbers; or omit the panel (prompt 4).
- **F · `asks.where`** — `/issues.asks` gains `where{en,id,es}` or `null`, computed by `engine._asks_ledger` from the stored `facility` rows through `packs/make/adapter.ask_line` — the pack owns the wording, the engine only calls it, and a distance is never re-derived (prompt 3, one commit). The page draws the facility row with or without the sentence; it never composes one.

Everything else on the plate has a field today. Per `docs/NEXT_RELEASE.md` rule 3 the page ships alone, so A, C and F go out in a node release before the page's, and the page draws its honest lines until a tester's node has them.

## 5 · Decisions this reading adds

1. **One vocabulary for the four distances.** `labels` say *room · yard · ring · region*; the sentences say *the wall outside · the street · the model*. The matrix headings and the meters should use one set. Default in the prompts: change `LABEL_WORDS` to the sentence nouns (`room · wall outside · street · model`, with `id`/`es` to match) so a heading and the sentence under it agree; the API keys stay `room/yard/ring/region`.
2. **The barcode's grain**: hourly today, or add `step=15m` (B).
3. **Filter life as the first Act-side capacity line** (`xiaomi-air`), ahead of `capacity.yml` (19 Sep act-side note §5).
4. **Where the `make` line lives** (new). Default: in Act, as a `row` under the open ask it answers — the pack's own `rules.yml` says the line belongs to other packs' asks — never a freestanding tile, never on the lead, never counted as a station; drawn only from what the node stores and says; the provenance word is `cached` with the snapshot filename as its date; when the pack is off the section says so in the node's words, switch and reason included. The sketch does not yet draw this row; it is added in the next round of the artifact, in the Act stage, as a one-line row under the open ask.
5. **The care label** may gain a seventh refusal in the pack's words (places, not numbers). Tomas's call; the prompts do not assume it.
